About this demonstration

This is a generic calculator concept for review of the proposed Option 2 website. The interface is inspired by simple dose-volume calculators and a pump-style infusion workflow. It is not affiliated with any pump manufacturer. A drug-specific calculator and clinical rules belong to a separate Option 3 scope.

All calculation requirements, units, edge cases, rounding, clinical safeguards, and deployment must be reviewed and independently validated by authorized clinical and IT teams before any clinical use.
